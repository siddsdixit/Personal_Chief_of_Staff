Personal Chief of Staff

Trust velocity, not busier. An AI system that remembers what your morning brain will forget.

A hotel desk at 6:40. Pleasanton some weeks. New York others. Same ritual either way.

Calendar. Email. Slack. Ramp. Jira. Sometimes Confluence when a decision had been buried and you were the last to find it. Coffee cooling while you told yourself you were catching up. Approvals. Hiring loops. Board prep. Reviews.

Busy in five systems and still late to the thing that would matter by noon.

The Problem

Not inbox zero. Trust velocity.

The misses never announced themselves as crises. A small ask sat past a day. A counterpart learned about a call after the thinking had already closed. A spend approval waited until it became furniture. Separately, each one was easy to forgive. Together they are how people stop believing you will close the loop.

Hopping tabs feels like leadership when it is only motion. The cost shows up later, in rooms you care about. A peer who stopped looping you in early because you were hard to close with. A manager who does not need you to be busier. They need you to be reliable on the small asks that protect credibility.

You needed a system that would not let open loops go quiet while you hopped tabs and called the hopping work.

What's Inside

Morning Brief

One page instead of five half-truths. Hottest carried-over asks on top. Calendar prep. Mail and Slack as drafts, not sends. Unanswered asks from last week surfaced.

Drafts in Your Voice

Every email and Slack message lands in drafts. Your gate. Your voice. No autonomous sends that create new mess. The routine reads how you actually write.

The Ledger

State that survives between 7am and 5pm. Open items get a first-seen date that never rewrites. Age turns to heat. Closures move to archive. Silence does not count.

Sunday Report

One number you cannot dismiss: is your loop-closing getting better or worse, and with whom. Exactly one recommendation. It is allowed to say the trend is bad. That permission is why you come back.

Hourly Pulse

Through the day, a check on the next hour so meetings do not introduce themselves by your walking in cold. Meeting prep. Email drafts. Slack pulse.

Everything is Markdown

No build pipeline. If you cannot read the rules, you do not have an operating model. You have a mood. Audit what you ship.

Five Triggers + Nine Commands

Scheduled Routines (Cloud-Triggered)

  • Morning Briefing (7:00 AM)
  • Hourly Pulse (during work hours)
  • EOD Summary (5:45 PM)
  • Evening Urgent Check (9:00 PM)
  • Weekly Pattern Report (Sunday 7:00 PM)

On-Demand Commands (Any Time)

  • cos scan - full status check
  • cos-brief - decision brief from email/Slack/calendar
  • cos-delegate - task + Jira ticket + logbook
  • cos-find-time - mutual availability
  • cos-focus - book focus time blocks
  • cos-followup - nudge tracking on asks
  • cos-thread - summarize Slack threads
  • cos-spend - spending overview (Ramp)
  • setup-cos - guided 8-phase interview setup

Build Your Seat

The payoff is not a prettier morning. It is still being trusted in the rooms that decide your year.
